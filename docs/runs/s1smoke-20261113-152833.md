## Problems / observations
1. **L40S GPU check** (job 152821, `cluster/scripts/gpu_check.py`): driver 550.54.14, fp32 matmul, Adam and the trainer imports pass.
   - The cuDNN GRU check "fails": max |GPU−CPU| is 4.2e-4 against a 1e-4 limit.
   - A follow-up (job 152824) shows this is **TF32**: with `torch.backends.cudnn.allow_tf32=False` the error is 5.5e-6 (as on the A100), and with PyTorch's default (`True`) it is 3.1e-4.
   - TF32 is normal reduced-precision math for training, so the L40S is fine for the trainer. The A100's cuDNN happened to pick a non-TF32 GRU kernel.
   - Files: `~/beanRant/runs/l40s-gpucheck-20261113/`.
2. **gpul40q scheduling:** the nodes are shared (57 of 64 cores in use).
   - `-c 6` with 1 GPU was scheduled for the next day, although 7 cores and 2–3 GPUs were free on each node; `-c 4` started at once.
   - The likely cause is core binding of the GPUs to sockets: `--gres-flags=disable-binding` didn't help.
   - Size jobs with `sbatch --test-only` first.
3. The trainer prints the cosmetic `_worker` tracebacks at shutdown, as before (D-029; [S0 smoke](s0smoke-20261112-152030.md), observation 4).
